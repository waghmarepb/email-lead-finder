# Lead scoring rubric

Add points; cap at 100. Then map to a tier.

## Intent (max 40)
- Explicit request for a quote / proposal / "can you build X": +40
- Clear problem the user can solve, but no direct ask: +25
- Vague interest, networking, or "let's connect": +10

## Fit (max 25)
- Request matches a service listed in `my-services.md`: +25
- Adjacent to those services: +10
- Outside services, or listed under "Not interested in": +0

## Company signal (max 20)
- Verified business domain with a real company behind it: +15
- Company is in a sector listed under "Sectors I already serve" in `my-services.md`: +5
- Personal email with no identifiable business: +0

## Commercial signals (max 15)
- Budget mentioned: +8
- Timeline or urgency mentioned ("by next month", "urgent", "ASAP"): +5
- Decision-maker title (founder, owner, director, CEO, MD): +2

## Penalties
- Thread dormant 30+ days with no reply from them: -15
- Looks like a reseller/agency pitch to the user rather than a buyer: -20
- Request is for free work / "exposure": -25

## Tiers
- **Hot (70–100):** reply today
- **Warm (40–69):** reply this week, qualify budget/timeline
- **Cold (1–39):** low priority; keep an eye on it

Always give a one-line reason that names the main factors, e.g. "Clinic owner, asked for booking-app quote, budget mentioned, wants it by Jan."
